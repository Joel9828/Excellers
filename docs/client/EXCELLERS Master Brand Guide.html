05

Imagery & pattern

PhotographyReal people collaborating, leaders in context, architecture and technology at human scale. Cool natural light, Marian tint in shadows, room for type. No handshake stock.
DuotoneMarian → Cyan for abstract sections.
AvoidClip-art, neon filters, busy backgrounds, globes and circuit clichés.

Pattern family

Open-joint meshThe brand texture. Corners and edges, never behind body text or as an icon frame.
Density gradientLines thickening into solid blocks. Backgrounds and progress; one direction per layout.
Dot rhythmDots swelling into dot-and-bar links. Dividers and loaders.
06

Iconography

Minimal outlined line icons built from junctions and dots. Strokes meet at 120° or run at 0°, 60°, 90°, 120°. Dots mark joints; exactly one Pacific Cyan “manifest dot” sits where the idea becomes real.

E-Principle cluster

Seven honeycomb positions. Hollow ghosts are possibilities; each stage connects more, and dots added in that stage are Cyan. For the E-Principle only.

Specifications

Voice & messaging

Confident, not loudState outcomes plainly.
Clear, not simple-mindedShort sentences; define jargon once or cut it.
Action-ledVerbs first: manifest, build, deliver, evolve.
HumanWarm, collaborative, “we” with the client.
ContextToneExample
Website heroBold, visionary“Your vision, delivered.”
SocialEnergetic, concise“Strategy is easy to write. Execution is where ventures are made.”
ProposalsFormal, evidenced“We recommend a three-phase programme with measurable outcomes at each gate.”
SupportCalm, accountable“We have reproduced the issue and will update you by 3 pm.”
CareersInviting“Help us turn ambition into results.”
  • Lead with the client’s outcome
  • Active voice, concrete numbers
  • “We Manifest” with both words capitalised
  • Paragraphs of three sentences or fewer
  • Buzzwords: synergy, disruptive, best-in-class
  • Over-promising or guarantees
  • Body copy in all caps
  • Robotic or overly casual tone
Boilerplate: EXCELLERS is a business transformation consultancy that helps individuals and organisations excel and achieve. Through strategic planning, effective execution and dedicated effort, we turn bold visions into resilient, successful ventures.
404: This page hasn’t manifested yet. Head back home and we’ll get you moving.

Where the source documents disagreed

  • Icon container: Brand Guidelines v1.1 allowed an optional hexagon outline; Icon Guidelines v4.0 bans hexagon badges. This guide follows v4.0.
  • Clear space: 9pt here; the source also shows 12pt / 11.8pt on one page. Confirm the final value with the logo designer.
  • Constantia: labelled both primary and secondary in the source; treated here as the primary display face.
  • Cyan on icons: v1.1 said “one optional detail”; v4.0 makes exactly one manifest dot mandatory for brand icons.
