02

Visual identity system

Logo

Two parts: the symbol (a stylised horse integrated into the "C") and the Bodoni MT logotype. Always use the supplied files. Never redraw, recreate or substitute.

VersionsPrimary: full-colour gradient on light, white on Marian Blue. Secondary one-colour: black or white only, for colour or photographic backgrounds. On light backgrounds prefer full-colour.
Clear space9pt on all four sides of the logotype, minimum. Keep other marks, text and edges outside it. (The source guide also shows 12pt / 11.8pt on one page; confirm the final value with the designer.)
Minimum sizeMain logo 111×35px on screens. Below 9px height use the small-size logo. Symbol alone: 10×13.1px minimum.
Trademark symbolUse only as the full gradient symbol with "We Manifest". Do not crop the logotype, and do not use it as a social profile avatar.

Incorrect usage

  • Distort or stretch
  • Use outlines
  • Change the typeface
  • Use non-corporate colours
  • Add elements or shadows
  • Crowd the clear space
  • Frame it in a shape
  • Change symbol/logotype relation
  • Fill with images or patterns
  • Rotate or incline
  • Place on low-contrast backgrounds

Imagery & photography

SubjectReal people collaborating, leaders in context, architecture and technology at human scale. No generic handshake stock.
LookCool, natural light with a Marian Blue tint in shadows. Clean compositions with room for type. Duotone Marian→Cyan for abstract sections.
PatternThe honeycomb is the brand texture: thin hexagon outlines in tinted blue-lavender, used at the corners and edges, never behind body text.
AvoidClip-art, neon-saturated filters, busy backgrounds, globes or circuit clichés.
Iconography research & system

Icon language

What the brand already says. The guide's recurring shape is the hexagon (honeycomb patterns, palette swatches, cover artwork), set beside a stylised, single-stroke horse symbol. Hexagons read as structure, efficiency and collaboration; the horse reads as drive and forward motion. Icons should extend the hexagon, not compete with the horse, which stays reserved for the trademark.

Grid & stroke24px grid, 2px stroke, rounded caps and joins, no fills. Matches the thin honeycomb line weight.
ColourMarian Blue stroke on light, white on dark. One optional Pacific Cyan detail per icon, never more.
ContainerOptional hexagon outline in Honolulu Blue (1.5px). Use for feature and E-Principle icons; omit in dense UI.
RulesMetaphors must be literal and quick to read. No gradients in icons, no horse glyph, no mixed styles from other libraries.

Starter set: the E-Principle

Extend with the same rules for services, UI and social. Commission or select any third-party set only if it can be restyled to these specs.

03

Voice & messaging framework

Confident, not loudState outcomes plainly. Let evidence carry the weight.
Clear, not simple-mindedExplain complexity in short sentences. Define jargon once or cut it.
Action-ledVerbs first: manifest, build, deliver, evolve.
HumanWarm, collaborative, "we" with the client.

Tone by context

ContextToneExample
Website heroBold, visionary"Your vision, delivered."
Social mediaEnergetic, insightful, concise"Strategy is easy to write. Execution is where ventures are made."
Proposals & reportsFormal, precise, evidenced"We recommend a three-phase programme with measurable outcomes at each gate."
Client supportCalm, responsive, accountable"Thanks for flagging this. We have reproduced the issue and will update you by 3 pm."
CareersInviting, purpose-driven"Help us turn ambition into results."

Do

  • Lead with the client's outcome
  • Use active voice and concrete numbers
  • Write "We Manifest" with both words capitalised
  • Keep paragraphs to three sentences

Don't

  • Use buzzwords (synergy, disruptive, best-in-class)
  • Over-promise or use guarantees
  • Set body copy in all caps (uppercase is for display headlines and buttons only)
  • Sound robotic or overly casual

Sample copy

Homepage headline: From Possibilities to Progress. We help organizations turn opportunities, challenges and ideas into clearer decisions, stronger systems and measurable progress.
Boilerplate: EXCELLERS is a business transformation consultancy that helps individuals and organisations excel and achieve. Through strategic planning, effective execution and dedicated effort, we turn bold visions into resilient, successful ventures.

Digital & print applications

WebsiteWhite or Mist canvas with Marian Blue inversion bands. 12-column grid, max width ~1200px, 8px spacing scale. One H1 per page (Constantia). Cyan only for primary actions and key highlights. Honeycomb at corners, logo top-left with full clear space, buttons 48px high.
Social templates1080×1350 and 1080×1080. Marian Blue or white background, Constantia headline, Inter support line, honeycomb in one corner, logo bottom-left. One idea per post. Profile avatar: use a dedicated avatar, not the trademark symbol.
Business card85×55mm. Front: white, full-colour logo, safe zone respected. Back: Marian Blue, name in Constantia, details in Inter 8pt+, honeycomb corner.
Letterhead & documentsA4/US Letter, 20mm margins, logo top-left, footer with "We Manifest" and web address, thin Marian Blue rule. Body Inter 10–11pt print; headings Constantia.
PresentationsCover on Marian Blue with white logo; content slides white. Headline Constantia 36pt+, body Inter 16pt+, footer bar with page number.
AccessibilityBody text contrast at least 4.5:1. Never put Cyan text on white. Use Marian Blue for text and reserve Cyan for fills and lines.
05 · Website content system

From Possibilities to Progress

The website is a twelve-section narrative that moves the reader from tension (possibility is not progress) to method (the E-Principle) to proof to action. Every page should reuse this logic and vocabulary.

Content building blocks

Capabilities (8)Business & Strategy · Customer Experience · Operations · People · Technology · Digital Growth · Industry Solutions · AI & Automation. Always in this order, numbered 01–08, one descriptive line each.
E-Principle (5)Explore: What is possible? · Enlighten: What matters? · Execute: What needs to happen? · Empower: Who carries it forward? · Evolve: What comes next? Each stage is a question plus one line.
Audiences (5)Founders, Leaders, Operations teams, Growth teams, Transformation teams. Pattern: "Turn [friction] into [progress]."
Engagements (3)Advisory (for a decision) · Partnership (for ongoing progress) · Transformation Program (for meaningful change). Every engagement includes a named lead, written roadmap, clear scope and terms, confidentiality, and capability transfer where required.

Headline & copy patterns

HeadlinesShort declaratives, often a reframe ("Possibility is not progress") or a verb-led promise ("We turn complexity into a way forward"). Set uppercase on web as display style only. Under 10 words.
Triads & laddersUse sets of three or five with a title and a single line. Keep titles one word where possible (Clarify, Build, Advance).
EyebrowsNumbered monospace-style labels ("01 — Hero"). Use sparingly and in sequence.
SentencesOne idea per line. Fragments are allowed for rhythm ("New markets. New technologies."). Body paragraphs stay within three sentences.

Uppercase, arrow suffix, Marian Blue fill with white text for primary; outline for secondary. One primary CTA per viewport.

Claims & integrity rules

  • Show only real proof: live systems, methods and approved case studies
  • Keep pricing language as "scoped around the work"
  • Keep confidentiality as a stated baseline
  • Use "We Manifest™" with the ™ on first and headline use
  • Publish placeholder or "reserved slot" copy
  • Invent client names, metrics or testimonials
  • Quote fixed prices or guarantees
  • List specific office or delivery claims beyond approved wording ("a flexible delivery model")
